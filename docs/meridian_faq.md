# Meridian Coffee Roasters — Frequently Asked Questions

## Where do you ship?
We ship to all 50 US states. International shipping is available to Canada and
the UK only. Orders over $45 ship free within the continental United States.

## How is my coffee packaged?
All coffee ships in resealable, foil-lined bags with a one-way degassing valve
to preserve freshness. The roast date is printed on the bottom of every bag.

## Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes. Cafes and offices ordering more than 20lb per month qualify for wholesale
pricing. Contact wholesale@meridianroasters.example for a rate sheet.

## What is your loyalty program?
The Meridian Circle rewards program gives 1 point per dollar spent. 100 points
can be redeemed for a free 12oz bag. Points expire 12 months after they are
earned.

## How should I store my coffee?
Store beans in an airtight container away from light, heat, and moisture. Do not
refrigerate or freeze opened coffee — condensation degrades the beans. Whole
beans stay fresh for about 4 weeks after the roast date; ground coffee for about
1 week.

## Do you use single-use plastic?
No. Our bags are recyclable, and all shipping boxes are made from post-consumer
recycled cardboard. Packing material is compostable cornstarch.

# Meridian Coffee Roasters — Product Catalog

## Harbor Blend
Our flagship medium roast. Notes of milk chocolate, toasted almond, and a soft
citrus finish. Grown in the Huila region of Colombia. Available in 12oz and 2lb
bags. Roast level: medium. Best for drip and pour-over.

## Midnight Reserve
A dark roast with a bold, smoky profile. Notes of dark chocolate, molasses, and
cedar. A blend of Sumatran and Brazilian beans. Roast level: dark. Best for
espresso and French press. Contains the highest caffeine content in our lineup.

## Meadowlight Decaf
A Swiss Water Process decaffeinated light roast. Notes of honey, green apple, and
jasmine. Single-origin Ethiopian. 99.9% caffeine-free. Roast level: light.

## Cold Brew Concentrate
A ready-to-dilute concentrate made from a coarse-ground blend of Harbor Blend.
Dilute 1:1 with water or milk. Shelf-stable unopened; refrigerate after opening
and use within 14 days. Sold in 32oz bottles.

All whole-bean products are roasted to order and shipped within 48 hours of the
monthly roast date.

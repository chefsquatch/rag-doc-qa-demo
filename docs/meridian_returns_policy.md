# Meridian Coffee Roasters — Returns & Refunds Policy

Meridian Coffee Roasters accepts returns on unopened whole-bean and ground
coffee within **30 days** of the delivery date. Opened bags are eligible for a
one-time **freshness guarantee** replacement within 14 days if the customer is
unsatisfied with the roast.

To start a return, email support@meridianroasters.example with the order number.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** of
the returned item arriving at our Portland warehouse.

Subscription orders can be paused or cancelled at any time before the monthly
roast date, which is the **1st of each month**. Cancellations after the roast
date apply to the following month's shipment.

We do not accept returns on brewing equipment that has been used, but unused
equipment in its original packaging may be returned within 30 days.
